Practice Exam Generator

Build randomized practice exams for risk management and insurance courses. Choose your university, course, and professor, then select the topics you want to practice — the generator assembles a randomized multiple-choice exam (with answer key) as a printable PDF, drawn from your professor's own test bank.

The practice exam generators are provided by The Emmett J. Vaughan Institute of Risk Management & Insurance in the Tippie College of Business at the University of Iowa. The Vaughan Institute prepares students for careers in risk management and insurance through its undergraduate RMI certificate and coursework taught by dedicated faculty.
